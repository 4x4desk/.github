# 4x4

**Your desk, your apps, your rules.**

4x4 is a desktop where every app is cut down to its essence: just the parts you need, with the ads, the tracking and the bloat taken out. Each app is one folder on your own computer that you, or your AI agent, are free to read and change.

- **4x4 AWD Desk** runs on the Mac, Windows or Linux you already have: [4x4desk.app](https://4x4desk.app)
- **Essence Applications** are open source apps and everyday sites, slimmed down and built from shared parts, so nothing is duplicated and everything can be updated in one place.
- **Open, all of it.** Code under AGPL-3.0, everything else under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Helping
The easiest way in is one app: take an open source app you like, whittle it down to its essence, and send it in. More on this soon.
